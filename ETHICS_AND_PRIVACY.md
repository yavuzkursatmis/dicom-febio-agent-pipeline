# Ethics and Privacy

> **MANDATORY PRE-SUBMISSION COMPLETION WARNING**

The following information must be completed and verified before manuscript submission or release claims are finalized:

- ethics committee/institutional review body name;
- approval date and decision/protocol number;
- informed-consent status or consent-waiver basis;
- retrospective hospital-archive access authorization;
- DICOM de-identification/anonymization procedure;
- data-retention and access-control rules;
- restrictions on data and derived-artifact sharing.

Until these fields are completed, this repository does not make a final claim of ethics approval or full anonymization.

No re-identification attempt is permitted.
